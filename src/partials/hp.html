F.A.Q.

Clear answers before we start

  • Which HP laptop is best for business?

    It depends on the employee's role and workload. HP ProBook models can be a practical choice for everyday business use, while EliteBook devices are designed for more demanding corporate and mobile scenarios. Specialists who require higher compute or graphics performance may need an HP ZBook mobile workstation.

    Neola can help define the appropriate configuration instead of selecting devices only by model or price.

  • Can you create standard HP configurations for different employee roles?

    Yes. We can define several hardware profiles for groups such as office employees, managers, mobile professionals, developers, designers, or engineers.

    This helps reduce the number of different configurations used across the company and makes future procurement easier.

  • Can Neola provide a complete workplace rather than just a laptop?

    Yes. A workplace can include the computer, one or more monitors, docking station, keyboard, mouse, headset, webcam, adapters, and other required peripherals.

    We select the components together to ensure that the complete setup matches the user's requirements.

  • Do you supply HP workstations?

    Yes. We can select HP Z desktop workstations and ZBook mobile workstations for engineering, design, visualization, development, data processing, and other demanding professional workloads.

  • Can you help us choose between several HP configurations?

    Yes. Tell us what applications your employees use, how they work, and what requirements or budget constraints you have. We can compare appropriate options and prepare a practical configuration for your project.
