Microsoft 365 Licenses & Plans: How to Choose the Right One

Microsoft offers several plan tiers. The right choice depends on company size, security needs, whether you need desktop Office apps, and other factors specific to your business.

Microsoft 365 Business Basic

Corporate email, Microsoft Teams, and browser-based document access — without desktop Office apps. A fit for team members who are fine with web versions or work mostly in Teams and Outlook.

Up to 300 users
Recommended

Microsoft 365 Business Standard

Everything in Basic, plus full desktop apps: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and more. The optimal choice for most office employees.

Up to 300 users
Recommended

Microsoft 365 Business Premium

Business Standard plus advanced security: Microsoft Entra ID P1, Microsoft Intune, and Microsoft Defender for Business. For companies that need device control, conditional access, and corporate data protection.

Up to 300 users

Microsoft 365 E3

For organizations with more than 300 users or advanced compliance and management requirements. The full toolset with no company-size limits.

300+ users

Microsoft 365 E5

Everything in E3, plus built-in security analytics (Microsoft Defender XDR), advanced compliance, voice capabilities, and Power BI Pro. For companies with elevated security and analytics requirements.

300+ users

Worth knowing: most companies have different roles — and those roles need different plans. There's no reason to pay for Business Premium across the board if part of the team only needs Basic. NEOLA builds the optimal license mix for your specific org structure.
