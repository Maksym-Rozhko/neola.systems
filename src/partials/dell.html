F.A.Q.

Clear answers before we start

  • Which Dell laptops are best for office employees?

    For most everyday business scenarios, Dell Pro laptops are a strong starting point. The right configuration still depends on the role, mobility, number of applications, display requirements and company standards, so we assess the workload before recommending a setup.

  • What is the difference between Dell Pro and Dell Pro Max?

    Dell Pro covers systems for mainstream business, office and hybrid work. Dell Pro Max is aimed at more demanding workloads such as design, graphics, large files, specialist applications and selected AI workloads.

  • When do you need a Dell Pro Max workstation?

    When a standard PC becomes the performance bottleneck — for example with CAD and 3D, video editing, large datasets, local AI workloads or software that needs stronger CPU, GPU or memory resources.

  • Can you build Dell configurations for hybrid work?

    Yes. A typical hybrid setup combines a Dell Pro laptop with a docking station, monitor and appropriate peripherals so the employee remains mobile but has a complete desk environment in the office or at home.

  • Can NEOLA standardize different workplace types?

    Yes. We can define a small set of standards for everyday office users, mobile employees, Power Users and professional teams instead of buying one identical device for everyone or selecting hardware ad hoc.

  • Can you help refresh an existing Dell device fleet?

    Yes. We can plan a phased refresh for devices approaching warranty expiry or no longer meeting performance requirements, select suitable replacements and roll them out gradually to reduce disruption.

  • Should AI PC and Copilot+ PC requirements be considered now?

    Yes, if your organization expects to use newer on-device AI functions. In that case, processor, NPU, memory and expected lifecycle can be included in the configuration standard from the start.
