F.A.Q.

Clear answers before we start

  • What services does Neola provide?

    Neola provides cloud services, cybersecurity, networking, IT infrastructure, and workplace technology solutions for business. We help design, deploy, migrate, secure, and support IT environments.

  • Can you help with Microsoft 365, Azure, or AWS?

    Yes. We help companies plan and implement Microsoft 365, Azure, and AWS solutions — from tenant setup and migration to security, infrastructure, and ongoing support.

  • Do you only provide consulting?

    No. We combine consulting with practical implementation. Our team can help assess your needs, design the solution, deploy it, configure the environment, and support it after launch.

  • Can you work with existing IT infrastructure?

    Yes. We can review your current environment, identify risks or limitations, and recommend improvements without forcing a full replacement where it is not needed.

  • Which vendors do you work with?

    We work with trusted technologies from Microsoft, AWS, Fortinet, HPE, Aruba, Veeam, Dell, Lenovo, Apple, and other established vendors.

  • Can you help select hardware, licenses, and subscriptions?

    Yes. We can help maintain, optimize, secure, and scale your IT environment after the project is completed.

  • Do you provide support after implementation?

    Usually, we start with a short discovery discussion. We clarify your business needs, current IT environment, risks, goals, and then propose the next practical steps
