F.A.Q.

Clear answers before we start

  • How is Zerto different from backup?

    Backup ensures that a copy of the data exists, while Zerto focuses on restoring services quickly and minimizing data loss through Continuous Data Protection and journal-based recovery. Zerto replicates changes in near real time and helps bring services back online with minimal data loss. The two technologies complement rather than replace each other.

  • Why does a business need Zerto?

    To reduce downtime for critical systems and minimize the risk of data loss after an outage, human error, ransomware attack, or site failure. Zerto provides fast disaster recovery and enables organizations to verify that their recovery plan actually works.

  • What are RPO and RTO?

    RPO defines how much data the business can afford to lose - in other words, how far back in time recovery may need to go. RTO defines how quickly services must be restored. These are two key metrics in any DR plan and the starting point for our design process.

  • Can Zerto help after a ransomware attack?

    Yes. Replication with point-in-time recovery makes it possible to restore systems to a state before encryption and reduce the impact of the attack. This is not attack prevention; it is a recovery capability after an incident.

  • Can disaster recovery be tested without stopping production?

    Yes. Recovery testing is performed in an isolated environment, so production services continue running while you verify that the DR plan will work when needed.

  • Does NEOLA help design the entire DR architecture?

    Yes. We work not only with Zerto implementation but with the complete DR architecture: critical systems, RPO/RTO, networking, access, the target recovery site, testing, documentation, and ongoing support.
