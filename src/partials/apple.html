F.A.Q.

Clear answers before we start

  • Which Apple devices should a business choose?

    It depends on roles and working scenarios. Some employees need a MacBook or Mac for everyday or creative work, others benefit from iPad for mobile workflows, while iPhone provides communication and access to corporate services away from the office. We define the right mix after reviewing the roles.

  • When is a MacBook a better choice than an iPad?

    A MacBook is suited to a full workplace with broader application use and heavier daily workflows. iPad is useful for mobile scenarios such as presentations, field and service work, warehouses, training and sales. Depending on the role, the two can also complement each other.

  • Can Mac, iPad and iPhone be managed centrally?

    Yes. With MDM and Apple Business Manager, devices can be enrolled, configured and managed centrally, including security policies, applications, restrictions and remote administration.

  • What is Apple Business Manager used for?

    It supports centralised preparation and enrolment of Apple devices, users, applications and content in a business environment. Together with MDM, it helps create a controlled process for deploying and maintaining devices.

  • Do Apple devices work with Microsoft 365 and Teams?

    Yes. Mac, iPad and iPhone can work with Microsoft 365, Teams, email and other corporate services. We help configure access and policies so Apple devices fit into the company’s working environment.

  • Can Apple devices be deployed in a Windows-based company?

    Yes. Apple devices can be integrated into a mixed environment through MDM, Microsoft 365 and other corporate services. The company does not need to move every user or system to a single platform.

  • Can NEOLA standardise Apple devices across different employee groups?

    Yes. We can define a small number of clear configurations for office, mobile, management, creative, sales and service roles, then align device management through Apple Business Manager and MDM.

  • Can different Apple bundles be created for different roles?

    Yes. Each employee group can have its own device and accessory bundle instead of receiving the same setup regardless of how they work.
