F.A.Q.

Clear answers before we start

  • Which Lenovo laptops are suitable for office employees?

    It depends on the role. For many office scenarios, a business ThinkBook or ThinkPad is sufficient; mobile users may prioritise weight and battery life, while managers may place more value on display quality and portability. We define the configuration after reviewing the role.

  • What is the difference between ThinkPad and ThinkBook for business?

    ThinkPad is focused on business reliability, durability and manageability, and is often well suited to users who work extensively on the move. ThinkBook combines business functionality with a modern design and can fit office and hybrid roles where value and everyday usability are important.

  • When is a ThinkStation workstation required?

    When ordinary PC performance becomes a limitation for tasks such as engineering, 3D graphics, video work, large datasets or specialised applications. ThinkStation can then be configured around the specific workload.

  • Can Lenovo solutions support hybrid work?

    Yes. We can combine business laptops with docking solutions, monitors and peripherals so employees can work comfortably both at a fixed desk and away from the office.

  • Can NEOLA help standardise Lenovo workplaces?

    Yes. We define a small number of clear role-based standards so procurement, support and refresh cycles remain predictable rather than fragmented.

  • Can Lenovo equipment be selected for Teams and Zoom meeting rooms?

    Yes. ThinkSmart solutions can be selected around the room size and collaboration scenario, including Microsoft Teams and Zoom environments.

  • Can NEOLA help refresh an existing laptop and PC fleet?

    Yes. We can plan and deliver a gradual refresh, from replacement-model selection through supply and integration, without forcing the organisation into a disruptive one-time change.

  • Can different Lenovo bundles be created for different employee groups?

    Yes. Office users, mobile employees, managers, engineers and designers can each have a different workplace profile instead of receiving the same configuration.
